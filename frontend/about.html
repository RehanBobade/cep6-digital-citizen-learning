About the program

Digital services, understood safely

Digital Citizen 360 is a college learning project about using common Indian digital services with care, confidence and respect for personal information.

Learn. Understand. Use Digital Services Safely.

Explore DigiLocker, Aadhaar, PAN and digital safety through guided topics, sourced videos and knowledge checks. This is an educational demonstration, not a government service or official certificate.
