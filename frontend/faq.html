Help and guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

What is DigiLocker used for?

DigiLocker helps people access and share issued digital documents. Use the official service and verify every sharing request.

How can I safely manage Aadhaar services?

Use UIDAI's official website, share identity details only when necessary, and never disclose an OTP to a caller or message.

Where should I check PAN information?

Use the Income Tax Department's official website for PAN-related services and current instructions.

What should I do about a suspicious OTP or phishing request?

Do not respond or open its links. Independently contact the service through its official website and report suspected cybercrime through the official Indian reporting channels.

Is Digital Citizen 360 an official government service?

No. It is a college educational project and does not issue government documents or certificates.
